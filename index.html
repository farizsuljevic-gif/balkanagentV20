FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before ordering.

What happens after I order?

You receive payment instructions and an order reference. After the payment is confirmed, BalkanAgent contacts you to collect your business information and begin setup.

How long does setup take?

A basic website AI setup can usually begin within a few business days after we receive all required information. Complex integrations may take longer.

Can it work with WhatsApp?

Yes, depending on the selected package and the technical requirements of your WhatsApp Business setup. Integration details are confirmed during onboarding.

Which languages are supported?

The website currently presents English, German, Russian, Bosnian, Albanian, Macedonian, Slovenian, Bulgarian, Greek, Romanian and Turkish.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation and notice terms should be confirmed in your individual order or service agreement before activation.

Is payment automatic?

Not yet. Payments are currently made by SEPA bank transfer and service activation is handled manually after payment verification.
